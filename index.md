Welcome. This is my public knowledge base: the notes I write while learning networking, IT, and systems administration, and the full walkthroughs of the labs I build to practice them.

I'm Abran Lezama. I have a bachelor's degree in computer science and the CompTIA A+ and Cisco CCNA certifications. Writing things down in my own words is how I make sure I actually understand them, so everything here is written to be clear to someone learning the topic for the first time, including future me.

## What's here

### 📚 [Notes](notes/)

Explanations of core concepts, from how networks move data to how routing, switching, and network services work. Each note focuses on one idea and links to related ones, so you can follow a topic as deep as you want.

### 🧪 [Labs](labs/)

Step-by-step walkthroughs of the home labs I build with physical Cisco gear and simulated topologies. Each lab covers:

- **Objective:** what the lab is meant to prove
- **Topology:** the devices, addressing, and how they connect
- **Implementation:** the configuration, step by step
- **Verification:** the commands and outputs that show it works
- **Troubleshooting:** what broke and how I fixed it
- **Takeaways:** what I learned

Many labs link back to the notes that explain the theory behind them.

## How to use this site

- **Search** with the search bar to jump straight to a topic.
- **Follow links** inside notes to move between related ideas.
- **Open the graph view** to see how topics connect.
